# World Governance Validation Sponsorship Contract

World Governance may sponsor validation opportunities, but it may not grant
truth, trust, promotion, graduation, or governed validation success.

Core principle:

```text
Truth and Trust are earned through evidence.

Governance may open the road to truth,
but it may never grant truth itself.
```

Truth Preparation Gate:

```text
OPPORTUNITY_PERMISSION_ONLY
```

The gate means a capability may receive additional validation opportunities. It
does not mean the capability is promoted, trusted, true, or graduated.

Allowed World Governance outputs:

```text
validation_opportunity_priority
validation_opportunity_count
elite_validation_priority
capability_merit_score
ground_truth_priority
validation_sprint_allocation
cluster_validation_priority
cross_domain_validation_priority
```

Forbidden World Governance outputs:

```text
trust_score
truth_score
graduation_score
promotion_decision
truth_decision
governed_validation_decision
```

Immutable truth boundary:

```text
INVESTMENT_NEVER_INFLUENCES_TRUTH_FORMATION
MERIT_NEVER_INFLUENCES_TRUTH_FORMATION
VALIDATION_PRIORITY_NEVER_INFLUENCES_TRUTH_FORMATION
VALIDATION_SPONSORSHIP_NEVER_INFLUENCES_TRUST_FORMATION
```

Promotion philosophy:

```text
Capabilities are not promoted because they are important.
Capabilities are promoted because they produced sufficient evidence.
```

Importance may allocate opportunities. Evidence decides truth.
